Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Overview

This is a personal tool that uses rclone to transfer files to the owner's Google Drive account. It is not affiliated with Google.

Information and Access

The tool uses Google OAuth 2.0 to authorize access to Google Drive. Authorization tokens are stored by rclone on the owner's own device and are used to access Google Drive as authorized by the account owner.

How Information Is Used

Files selected by the owner are transferred to the owner's Google Drive. This website does not collect, store, or sell visitors' personal information.

Data Sharing

The tool does not sell personal information or share it with third parties for advertising. Google Drive access is subject to Google's privacy policy and the permissions granted by the account owner.

Security

OAuth credentials and tokens should be kept private and must not be published on this website.

Contact

For questions about this personal tool, contact the owner through the GitHub repository.
